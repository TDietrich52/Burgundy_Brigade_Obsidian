Notes From [[Ghost]]

PIBB has mentioned seeing colors, strange connections to a C.K,, and Miri shifted into *that thing*. DarkNight GrimReaper warned me that he didn’t want to get me involved for my own safety and I can begin to understand why now. Frankly however, he overestimates my interest in PIBB’s affairs. 

My only interest is to save those trapped within Project PA and the victims of Project RUST. They are all that matters to me right now. I tore two beings away from existence. And while PIBB promised to help save the robots - they seemed far too willing to leave such vulnerable creatures somewhere so dangerous. I trust DN, but I have seen how PIBB operates. They could easily fail, and I don’t believe the robots would be a meaningful loss to most of them. 

PIBB, DN, Ludwig Afton, and especially Harold Finch seem to all fail to understand. Death is simply a transitional state for those with a soul, we can exist for eternity on other planes. But for those who lack a soul it is the end. The concept is horrifying. And while death remains intimidating for mortals - this feeling is minimal when compared to those who simply disappear.  

I lost Jack. I can only take comfort in the fact he will exist with his god in some far away plane I will never get to see. But I know he is well and through that knowledge I will be ok.

And while I wait with my remaining time, there is only one way to truly help those who share my material plane. It is my duty now to protect those who do not have the same assurance I do. Those who do not share the luxury of an afterlife, those who will disappear if they are not kept safe.
