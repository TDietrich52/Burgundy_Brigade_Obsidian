Strange magic is a mysterious magical force found inherent in certain individuals. They manifest in bizarre uncharacteristic magical abilities not found in traditional schools of magic. While the exact causes of Strange Magic remain unknown. It often parallels those who interact heavily with ancient artifacts sourced from a powerful black opal like gemstone.
