Marvelous Many Mechanical Men

# Physical Location
M4's physical location in the [[Iron and Wine District]] boasts a cooperate building just as large as its factory building located right next door. The buildings are intentionally build to resemble a large mechanic body, with corporate higher ups working closer to the head as the ranks rise, and the factory making up the lower regions of the body.

This form is meant to be a tribute to the god of machinery [[Primus]], as confirmed by [[Harold Finch]].

### Dig Sights
Dig sites have been mentioned by the factory workers after [[DarkNight GrimReaper]] asked them about the location of [[Adymantine]]. These are located in the [[Grim Mountains]] and are specifically used for digging out rare materials.


# Pre-Union Revolt
The M4 company was the largest producer of automatons on this side of Burgundy. Never closing their doors, the company boasted about it's efficiency. It's union, [[The Revachol Union]], often ensured safety regulations were met and workers were treated will.

### Goals
- Automatons were going to be divvied up between all industries, including war.
- [[Project RUST]]
- [[The Titan Project]]
- [[Project PA]]


### Downfall
Upon the announcement of goals to fully automat their assembly, production, and management - fully eliminating the need for mortal workers- the [[The Revachol Union]] decided to protest.
