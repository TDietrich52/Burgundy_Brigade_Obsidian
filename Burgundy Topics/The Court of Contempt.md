The Court of Contempt is a classic tale in Burgundy with countless retellings throughout the ages. 

## The Plot
The Court of Contempt follows an “ambassador” to a kingdom sending letters to a general, a scribe, the queen, and the king, warning that there is a peasant uprising. Each member of the court comedically rejects the notion of a revolution happening.  Each is unique in their foolishness, the general too confidant in his army, the queen to confident in her husband, and the king underestimating the peasants.  At the end of the poem, the peasants successfully revolting, killing the whole court except for the ambassador.

### Modern Retellings
Modern retellings are countless. Some retellings replace the idea of a King with a mayor, or other similar variations. Recently, [[Jorge Washington]] wrote a retelling of this story that won at [[The Golden Mask Awards]]
