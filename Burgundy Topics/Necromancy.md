## Necromancy Basics
- Goal matters in necromancy - the desire will effect the outcome
- Phylactery and a ritual are needed to create a Lich
- Powerful necromancy casters needed to both perform the ritual and take on the role of the Lich.
- Phylactery Orbs
	The vessel for the soul of a lich 

## Necromancy in Burgundy 
### History of Necromancy in Burgundy
- Vecna was a lich who raised an army of undead thousands of years ago 
- Necromancy Experiments were Conducted by [[Professor Lynch]]
	[[Glim Mekkelferce]]'s Village who were killed by [[Panda Plague]]. Lynch tried to bring them back, succeeding, but they were in a twisted state 
### The Necromancy Ban In Burgundy
-   Deemed too dangerous so it was outlawed in the city
-  All bodies in Burgundy are required to be cremated to protect from necromancy
	- When someone dies, there is a check to see if they have a “Please cast Revivify” card. If not, they are given last rites, and cremated immediately
