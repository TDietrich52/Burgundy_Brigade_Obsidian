*(Robotic Utilities for Symbiotic Technicals)*

## General
This was a secret project conducted by the [[M4C (Revachol Industries)]], and spear headed by [[Ludwig Afton]]
*The stated goal by M4C was to "support biological functions by bionic means"*


## The Documentation
The Documentation on Project Rust was discovered by [[DarkNight GrimReaper]], [[Detective Bryan]], and [[Ghost]].

They uncovered small schematics for robotic limbs, organs, and jaws were uncovered with this project. But more importantly, was the story a test subject, a terminally ill [[Lain]] being given a new robotic body. 

A Handwritten note within the project was uncovered. Meant to be given to her once she was "functional". At the end, "Project Inconclusive: Subject Monitored"**


## [[Lain]]'s Connection with RUST
The RUST project had designed to "save" a [[Lain]], who was experiencing liver failure
* In actuality, RUST trapped her in a purgatory when her soul was removed from her body, in this time she was neither alive nor dead.
* Her consciousness was in the [[DreamScape]]


## Ending Project RUST

 After getting the coordinates to Project RUST, [[Luther Du'Mont]], [[DarkNight GrimReaper]], [[Ghost]], and [[Miriella Honza]] attempted to make an effort to uncover the girl. 
 
 In the year and a half since RUST had been attempted, hidden within the [[Grim Mountains]],  [[Abraham Callistus]] and several other doctors monitored [[Lain]] in an underground bunker, but all other employees had fled after the murder of [[Ludwig Afton]]. Abraham reported the bot could move but had limited abilities and needed to be charged for 5/6 hour periods.
 
 [[Miriella Honza]] was able to use her abilities to enter the [[DreamScape]] where communication with [[Lain]] was possible, and with the knowledge gained the [[Abraham Callistus]] was able to transfer Lain's soul back to her body.
