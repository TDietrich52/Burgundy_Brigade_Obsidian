## Before the Revachol Revolution
The Revachol Union served as a union to employees of [[M4C (Revachol Industries)]]. [[Opal]] served as the Vice President of the Union to the final president, [[Uzzuk Kyizm]], who was killed by a [[Project PA]] Prototype just before the Revolution.

## Post Revachol Revolution
The Revachol Union has seemingly taken over M4C, renaming the company to Revachol Industries.




## Trivia
- Revachol is a reference to a god of Machinery
