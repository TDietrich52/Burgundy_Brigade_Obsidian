## Panda Plague Overview
 -  Symptoms 
	 - Makes eyes sunken in and black and causes paling of the skin
-   Present day there are occasional odd cases but this is rare

## Creation of the Panda Plague
- A blight was draining the pine trees of their life force sometime around 6566.
- [[Professor Lynch]] created the [[Panda Plague]] to try and reverse the blight that was destroying the trees using [[Necromancy]] sourced magic. This failed.
