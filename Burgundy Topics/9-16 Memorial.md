Memorial dedicated to the souls who lost their lives on 9/16, during the [[Rat King Incident]].
	Built on the former site of [[Big Harbin]].
The original memorial was constructed by [[Lee Monet]] out of debris from the [[Miracle District]] and [[Heritage District]].
	The original momument was destroyed on 6/16 by [[Things]] in the [[Stufflock 2 Rat Thing Incident]].
	Plans for a new memorial are currently underway.
