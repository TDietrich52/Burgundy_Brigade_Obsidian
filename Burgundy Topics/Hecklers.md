News couriers from [[Old Burgundy (3669)]].

In the age before the printing press and the modern news cycle. The most prevalent way residents of Burgundy received news and alerts were through Hecklers. Hecklers were loud noticeable character that would scatter across various corners and squares of the city and scream verbal messages from city officials. Hecklers were used to cover a wider verity of topics from official decrees, a city wide threat, or just entertaining poems. 

These Hecklers would dress up in provocatively, eccentric clothing in order to drew eyer from crowds. They would sometimes use magic to enhance their messages as well, but it wasn't a standard for the practice. 

Hecklers feel out of fashion quite quickly following the invention of the printing press. Some long lineages of Hecklers known in Burgundy would later go on to be the founders of some of Burgundy's earliest news companies.
