When the [[Vultura]] discover that a member of their own order has jeopardized the unity of the [[Vultura]], they are not immediately killed.  Instead they are brought before the Vultura with a chance to "redeem" themselves.
This is known as The Last Hunt.
## What is the Last Hunt?
* It is claimed to date back to the Eileen and their days as a Wicker.
* The Last Hunt is a test of survival.
* It starts when you are brought into Vultura headquarters at dawn.
* You have until sunrise the next day to prove your loyalty to [[Wee Jas]] and the [[Vultura]] by finding and killing an undead and bringing their heart back to Vultura headquarters.
* During this time, every member of the Vultura will be authorized to eliminate you, by any means necessary.
* Failure to bring the heart of an undead back to Vultura Headquarters by sunrise will label you as an enemy of [[Wee Jas]].
	* You're personal information will be leaked to the press of Burgundy.
	* The Vultura will tell the [[Crow's Nest]] that you have been practicing resurrection, whether that be true or not, condemning you to death.
	* You will have wanted posters plastured in every city along the Rainbow Coast.
	* You will never be safe, until the day you die.
* Success does not mean you rejoin the [[Vultura]].
	* It means you get to leave the city of Burgundy with your life.
	* Should you return, your life will be considered forfeit to [[Wee Jas]].
* Most people given The Last Hunt understand that they are being sentenced to death and simultaneously stripped of their humanity, essentially reduced to prey running for its life.
* The Vultura believe that the Last Hunt is an essential element of their life, trimming the fat that aren't worthy and strengthening those that remain.
