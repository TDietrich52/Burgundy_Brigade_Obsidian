Secret Automaton project created by [[M4C (Revachol Industries)]], with the intentions of replacing all mortal workers.

Resulted in the creation of 75 sentient Porotype A automatons.
	Investigated by: [[PIBB]], [[Detective Bryan]], and [[Ghost]].
