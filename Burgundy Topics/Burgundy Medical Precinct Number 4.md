## Basic Overview
24 hour Medical Precinct operating in the [[Miracle District]] - that handles nonlethal emergencies mainly. The Precinct is part of an initiative to lessen the burden of [[Burgundy Medical Institute]] by having various urgent care medical precincts located throughout the whole city. Despite this thee Precint stays poorly funded. *(Medicine and medical treatment is socialized in Burgundy, but personal wealth can influence treatment level still)*

All Precincts come equip with a Morgue in order to cremate bodies.
	* See Burgundy's stance on [[Necromancy]] for more details
