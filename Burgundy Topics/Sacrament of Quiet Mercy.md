A religious practice and the final test of initiation for the [[Vultura]].
* To past this test, you must  kill a person for the sole purpose of bringing death, without alerting them to your presence and without leaving a trace, then returning to a specified location undetected with their heart.
	* If anyone sees your face, can trace you to the killing, detects foul play, or follows your trail, the test is considered failed.
	* If you fail the Sacrament of Quiet Mercy, then you're training is considered null, and you must prove yourself all over again, starting with finding headquarters.
		* Headquarters moves around and is thus incredibly difficult to locate.
	* You are allowed two failures in the Sacrament.
		* After your second failure you are considered unfit for serving [[Wee Jas]] and are banned from becoming a [[Vultura]].
		* Attempting to return to [[Vultura]] headquarters will result in your immediate execution.
