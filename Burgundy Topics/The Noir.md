"The Noir" is the colloquial term used along the Rainbow Coast to refer to the event of color being drained from the world.

As confirmed by [[Aline Harris]], The Noir was [[SCAB]]'s doing as an attempt to limit the [[Crimson King]]'s ability to manifest in the material plane by limiting the world to black and white.

Post the Noir, the people were still able to dream in color for a period of about nine months.
