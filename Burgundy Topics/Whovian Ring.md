## Basic
The Whovian Ring was a ring created by [[The Whovians]] and until recently was in [[Bordeaux Museum]]'s custody. Peering through the gem atop it allows for the user the see Burgundy in color during the era of [[The Noir]].


The Ring has since [[PIBB]] stole from the [[Bordeaux Museum]], and then stolen from them. It's current location is unknown.
