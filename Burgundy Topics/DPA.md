The DPA, or The Dagon Preparatory Academy of the Prosperous and Affluent, is a highly affluent boarding school located to the south of Burgundy. 

The student population is largely made up of the children's of highly affluent family's located along the Rainbow Coast. The school teaches 10-18 year old. The student body’s diversity in race and economic status are both non-existent, almost 90% seem to be incredibly wealthy elves, half elves, humans, and the occasional gnome.

# The DPA Incident 

In July of 6665, two seniors went disappeared. Abagail Larson and Percival Rogers went missing after going to a common couple's spot in the forest surrounding the school. After several days of no appearances, headmaster [[Theodore Bigsbee]], contacted [[PIBB]] to investigate the two student's whereabouts. 

Following a short investigation, the bodies of the two students were found mutilated nearby a pond in the neighboring forest. The DPA was locked down to guarantee the safety of the student body, however these efforts were fruitless. 

Fellow student [[Aurora Innsmouth]] had an intense crush on Percival, and after stalking his and Abagail to the forest killed the two in a jealous rage. This rage was ignited partially by her awakening to being a [[Deep Ones]]. She also came in possession of the [[Necronomicon]] shortly before this mysteriously. 

After being exposed as the killer, Aurora ran for the nearby coast of the Valguero. PIBB fought and killed her.
