Named after the [[Oleander Flower]] which only grows on this island. 

Oleander Island was once home to the largest prison complex on the Rainbow Coast. The island's isolated nature allowed passive security as well as its untouched Oleander Flower population. 


Was a very well established prison until a demon appeared, causing a "blood war" within the prison and its eventual closure. Afterwards [[SCAB]] took over to set up thier base here.
