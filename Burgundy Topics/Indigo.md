### Indigo Trivia
- Indigo has revolutionized transportation through both their Public Trams cars operated by [[Indigonese Tram Service (ITS)]] and Airships by [[Indigo Express Company]]  

- Indigo is named for its large deposits of Lapis Lazuli. 
		The city creates a vibrant blue light when it's structures are hit by light. Leading travelers to refer to the city as "The indigo city" or "Indigo". The beaches of Indigo also display this phenomenon when the tide scatters grains of lapis into the sand; called "An Indigo Tide".

- Indigo utilizes capital punishment for crimes such as necromancy, severe blasphemy, or worship of evil deities! 
	They have a list of banned Gods.

### Geography
Indigo rests on the coast of [[Lake Valguero]]. Bordered to the east by the Newarm Mountains ([[Grim Mountains]])
