## Chief Mog
After the disappearance of [[Edgar Soloman]], who was looking into Chief Mog ([[Mogwaii]]) and several other missing peoples cases, [[PIBB]] began go investigate the Police Station and stumbled into [[Doug Chaves]], attempting to convince his son to join him in using a elder brain to control the city. 

PIBB stopped Doug, and in their take down of the Chief and Doug, the second in command, essentially disbanded the entire police force.

## A New Era
