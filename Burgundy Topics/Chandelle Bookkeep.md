Do not call it a library, call it a bookkeep.  They are very elitist.


## Membership
#### Joining
 The Bookkeep requires a piece of knowledge worthy of it's archive and is not currently in the Bookkeep in order to become a member. 
#### Benefits
A pass to Chandelle Bookkeep will give you access to the various libraries that hold access points all across the rainbow coast, as well as anywhere in the Bookkeep itself.
-  Burgundy entrance is in [[Roi Cercle Library]]
- Confirmed entrance within [[Indigo]]


## History
The Chandelle Bookkeep was founded during [[Valtarian Wars]]. Much of the Rainbow Coast’s catalog of knowledge of the older world was either lost or in jeopardy of being lost. In response to this, a group of scholars from numerous cities from Valtis, Indigo, Burgundy, even as far north as Lilac all joined forces in creating a safe haven for knowledge.

#### The Founding
Pirates would then traffic the books out of [[Valtis]] after the [[Valtarian Empire]] declared the destruction of "dangerous knowledge".   A lot of the first knowledge preserved was of the old gods that Valtis was trying to ban.

Scholars hoping to protect their knowledge would use the cover of night to move their secret repositories of books, with the aid of candlelight. From there, books where moved onto a ship where a candle was used to signal to the scholar that knowledge was safe there.
-   These scholars became the first [[Chandelle Bookkeepers]].
-   [[Pratchett]] is the current Head of the Bookkeepers

One candle began to burn eternally, and that candle become [[Chandelle]].
-   This candle was moved into the Chandelle Bookkeep and became the anchor for the demi-plane.


## Current Operations
### The Chandelle Candle
-   The symbol of the ever burning Chandelle Candle is that of a candle which would direct a knowledge seeker towards the answer they seek.
-    The Bookkeep only exists as long as the light of the Candle touches it as it serves as the anchor to this demi plane.
