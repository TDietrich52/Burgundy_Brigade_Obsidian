This is an annual award ceremony celebrating the performance arts within Burgundy. Considered a very prestigious awards - however they are know to have issues with nepotism 

## The Award Categories
Best Performance: Acting category
Best Production: Best play/show
Best Orchestral Composition: Can be for writing a score, or composing. Either or really.
Best Playwright: For writing a play, musical or otherwise.
Best Scenic Design: For the set intricacies. Illusion magic is not always allowed as it feels cheaper at times. 
Best Musical Performance: For vocal performances. 

### The Silver Mask
This is an award given to a runner up at the Golden Mask Awards
