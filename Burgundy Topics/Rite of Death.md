Also called Ritual of Death Sight.
The final test a priest of [[Wee Jas]] must pass before they become a full fledged "Crow".
	The priest is put into a death-like trance in which they receive a vision of their death, followed by them being reborn through [[Wee Jas]].
	Sometimes the visions are clear, sometimes they are vague, sometimes their incredibly literal, other times they are extremely symbolic.  But no matter what the fate of the Crow is permanent in those visions.
	The idea behind the Rite of Death is to teach Crow's that while they may wield [[Wee Jas]]'s powers, they aren't invincible.  They must obey the ebb and flow of life and death as any other creature, to think themselves better is like being a man whose only known the desert battling the ocean.
		Even in death, they are all part of [[Wee Jas]]'s will, and no matter how hard they try to fight back, they cannot out run death.
	Not every Crow reacts well to the Rite of Death.
		Some go mad with grief.  Others become obsessed with bringing about a glorious end for themselves, while others do whatever it takes to outrun death.  Still others can become addicted to the trance they are put in, and become little more than living corpses.
When [[Glim Mekkelferce]] received her Rite of Death, she saw nothing.
	She feared this meant she had already been destined to die and thus her soul was forfeit to [[Wee Jas]], causing her to face complete oblivion and nothingness.
	In actuality, she received no vision because she was meant to serve as a prophet of [[Wee Jas]], enacting her will in the material plane before joining her side in the next life.
