The DreamScape Project is a research Project at [[BUAT]] pioneered by [[Dr. Stratus]] and [[Professor Talleron]], and assissted by [[Pierre Liquid]]


## Reason For Project
-   The moon demi-plane is not attached, it is encompassing over all of Burgundy.
-   This demi-plane was formed by a dream, there is some sort of temporal force looming over the city.
-   Talleron wants to make contact with this force.
-   This will no longer be planescaping, it is dreamscaping.


## Results of the Project
Upon using a spell circle built of tuning forks, it was possible for a small portal to be created. Through that a horrifying tentacle slipped out and at the same time - miles away- [[Miriella Honza]] became overwhelmed with pain in her head, seeing red, and was unable to shift out of her [[Deep Ones]] form. The project was put on a brief pause after this
