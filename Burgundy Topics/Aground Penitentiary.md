Since the evacuation of Oleander Prison, Burgundy has been repurposing old war and jail ships. 

Burgundy had a strong navy in the Nauticalia Era, however now in the Noir Era, much of that navy is out of commission or not needed. 

Following a drop in sea level for Lake Valguero, a small fleet of warships were beached on a sandbar. The city authorized these boats to be converted into an offshore prison facility. 
Aground Penitentiary is located around 40 miles offshore. A ferry ride to the mainland is scheduled 4 times a day to transport people and supplies, the ride will take around 2 hours.
