Estate of the Solomon Family located within the [[Grim Mountains]]

## The Incident
Group of individuals with high social statuses were invited to dinner at the Solomon Manor. They became trapped in a demiplane underneath the house. The Solomon family  was revealed to be evil and butler, Virion, a monster. Then family was killed by the group and Manor burned down.

The Solomon Family was survived by their son [[Edgar Soloman]] who was away at the [[DPA]].

## The Fallout
[[Professor Lynch]] was called by the Burgundy police to conduct a investigation to determine if necromancy was involved in the incident.
