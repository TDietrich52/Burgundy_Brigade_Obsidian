Splinter church from the Church of Wee Jas.


# Principles and Practices
## Core Beliefs
Central beliefs focus on [[Wee Jas]] as a goddess of death and themselves as the deliverers Wee Jas's will.
	This has left them to become increasingly specialized in the art of assassination, often achieving goals through political assassinations. 
	They believe that death comes for everyone, that all men must die, and that they shall bring about the death that [[Wee Jas]] has foretold.
Believed their mission was to bring about the death Wee Jas has had planned
in the centuries since the schism from [[Crow's Nest]].
## Becoming a Vultura
* The process to becoming a Vultura is not as extensive, but it is a lot more exclusive.
* The first step to becoming a Vultura is knowing a member or being scouted by one.
	* What Vulturans tend to look for in recruits are orphans, assassins, and those exposed to death constantly for whatever reason.
	* Their recruits are usually poor or desperate, or often join as a result of a personal vendetta that the law will not allow them to act on.
	* Sometimes zealots or Crows gone astray find their ranks in the Vultura.
* Once you are scouted, you are brought to their headquarters under the cover of night and begin training.
	* Vultura Training does away with practicing magics beyond the basics or studying history beyond the core tenets of the faith of [[Wee Jas]].
	* Instead, Vultura trainees are schooled in espionage, infiltration, sneaking, assassination, poisoning, detecting undead, hiding murderous intent, combat, mobility, endurance training, etc.
* The final test to earn your place among the Vultura is the [[Sacrament of Quiet Mercy]].
* Class Breakdown.
	* The vast majoirty of Vultura members are rogues and blood hunters.
	* Fighters, rangers, and monks are also extremely commonplace.
	* Paladin, bard, and warlock Vultura are somewhat uncommon but still noticeable, likely possessing their abilities BEFORE recruitment and training.
	* Artificers tend to not become trained assassins within the Vultura, but rather specialize in crafting unique gear for the Vultura to aid in their mission.
	* Clerics, wizards, and sorcerers are notably rare, magical knowledge is seen as a luxury in the Vultura, one few possess.
	* There are next to no Druid or Barbarian Vultura.  If you see one, they are one of a handful to ever exist.
## Failure and [[ The Last Hunt]].
* When a Vultura member is fails to uphold the tenets of [[Wee Jas]] or in any way endangers the safety of the Vultura, they are not banished or killed.
* Rather, they are captured and given one last chance at redemption, [[The Last Hunt]].

Recent History
=
### Sufjan's Coup
There was a power struggle, resulting in [[Valka Mortem]] being killed by [[Sufjan (The Corpse)]], who then took over as lead. Valka was then revived by [[Wee Jas]] but was jailed in Aground Penitentary.
Their goal at the time was to become death gods via the use of The Sacred Texts of Wee Jas.

## Current Status
The Vultura remain a very splintered group, partially due to the efforts of [[Magnus]] of seeking revenge after Sufjan's Coup.

The group now has roughly 20 members.
	Formerly, they numbered around 75 members.

## Dress Code
* Vultura do not have a set uniform in the same way the [[Crow's Nest]] does.
* Instead, Vultura dress with practicality for their mission in mind.
	* They still wear face coverings, and many choose to where skeleton masks to further hide their faces and intimidate their enemies
## Locations of the Vultura
* The current whereabouts of the Vultura is unknown.
* In the past, the Vultura operated out of a section of the [[Ratacombs]], using the cities sewers to sneak around unnoticed.
	* They never stuck to one place in the [[Ratacombs]] for long, making it extremely difficult for anyone but other Vultura agents to find it.
* During the reign of [[Sufjan (The Corpse)]], the Vultura were stationed within [[Fey Field Forest]].
## Assassination
* As a result of their beliefs and extensive training, Vultura are regarded as some of the greatest assassins on the Rainbow Coast.
* There is no one that they aren't able or willing to kill, for the right price.
	* While they do charge money for their services, the price they are infamous for is sacrifice.
* The Vultura believe that to request a life be taken, you must sacrifice something of equal value.
	* This applies to the richest of barons and the poorest of wretches, money is not enough, you need to be willing to sacrifice if you want to bring about death.
	* What is sacrificed depends on the individual.
		* For a rich man, it might be all the gold they've ever earned, or it could mean their eyesight.
		* For a poor woman, it could be a single penny, or it could mean something else.
		* For a father of three, it could mean the lives of one of his children.
	* What a person must sacrifice is determined by consulting with [[Wee Jas]].
* To hire the Vultura, you must be able to find one of their agents.
	* From there, give them a written note with the specifications of who you want killed, and they shall give you a location to meet them at a later date.
		* The agent will take the note back to headquarters, where it will be debated if the contract is worth fulfilling.
	* Afterwards, an agent will meet you at the specified location, and will tell you what must be sacrificed in order to complete this assignment.
		* If the client agrees to the terms, the contract is confirmed and will be honored to the best of the Vultura's capabilities.
		* If the client does not agree, then the contract is nulled and further communication with the Vultura is ended.
* Alternatively, Vultura can receive a vision from [[Wee Jas]] showing them someone who must be eliminated in service of their mission.
	* When this occurs, the Vultura right up their own contract and place it as the highest priority case.
* There is also an old wives tale that if you give a letter marked with blood to a bird of prey of some kind, then the Vultura will open a contract with you.
