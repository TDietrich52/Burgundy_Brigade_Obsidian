
## Deep One Biology
### According To Arthur
-   The [[Crimson King]] had some kids with eldritch blood, who had descendants
-   The kids are still running around, but only elves, dwarfs, and humans can become deep ones
-   Deep Ones are immortal, as in they don’t die of old age
-   The only way to kill the Crimson King is to kill all his descendants
-   The more people who know about the King, the more powerful it becomes and the more it manifests
-   The Necronomicon can trigger transformations in Deep Ones


## Awakening the Aberration
Experiences with both [[Aurora Innsmouth]] and [[Miriella Honza]] have confirmed that Deep Ones need to be awakened to their Aberration form.

[[Atticus McGraw (The Boffin)]] created a runic spell capable of forcing this form onto Deep Ones and Non-Deep Ones. [[Aurora Innsmouth]] was awakened with a similar rune found in her school journal.

## Upon Death Return to the King
Asmodeus has confirmed that Deep Ones upon death do not come to him as they are suppose to, and is unsure why.

This is confirmed by [[Richard Innsmouth (The Scripter)]] when he expresses his daughter is "in the grasp of the Crimson King"
