# [[The Noir]]
A battle ensued that saw [[PIBB]] kill all the Jesters, but not before they could complete their ritual, summoning forth the [[Crimson King]].
		It was later confirmed that [[SCAB]] stole the color from the world as means to prevent the Crimson King from entering the material plane, in event now known as [[The Noir]].

#### Trivia
