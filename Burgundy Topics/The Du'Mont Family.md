## Deceased Du'Mont Family Members
##### Walter Du'Mont Sr.
- Found of Du'Mont Shipping Incorporated (DSI), the legitimate business front of the Du'Mont Family
-  Great-grandfather of [[Luther Du'Mont]]

##### Walter Du'Mont Jr
- Former head of DSI
- Did significant amount of work to build a good reputation for the Du'Mont family in Burgundy
- Father of Lucy, Rufus, Clark, and Gregory Du'Mont
- Would often pit his children against each other
 - Left [[Castle Du'Mont]] to Luther, left Lucy out of the will entirely

##### Gregory Du'Mont
- Father of [[Luther Du'Mont]]
-  [[Lucy Du'Mont]] killed him (at the time it was thought to be a mob hit job)
- Former Head of Public Relations
	In charge with maintaining the criminal contacts the Du'Monts had
	Gregory and Walter Jr. clashed constantly and vocally

##### Sasha (Phillips) Du'Mont
-  wife of Gregory Du'Mont, mother of [[Luther Du'Mont]]
- 21 years old at time of death (died giving birth to Luther)
