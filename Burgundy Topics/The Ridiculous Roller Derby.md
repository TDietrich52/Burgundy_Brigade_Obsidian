# General
A yearly racing event hosted by [[The Ridiculous Roller Derby]]
Every year, people and ghouls from across the Rainbow Coast gather for the Roller Derby and a chance to win fabulous wealth and prizes
This year marks the 666th annual running of the Ridiculous Roller Derby, Hosted on July 4th 6666
[[Shaggy]] was once a participant in the Ridiculous Roller Derby
Created and hosted by [[Dragula Rayser]].
