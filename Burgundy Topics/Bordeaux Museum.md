Formerly curated by an [[Edvard (The Baron) Oslo]]. Oslo was revealed to be a famous black market artifact dealer nicknamed "The Baron". He had hired a group of thieves headed by [[Crackle]] the Kenku. 

The resulting confrontation between Oslo and the members of PIBB involved the destruction of much of the museum's interior as well as the skeleton of [[Brutus]] the ancient red dragon from the Grim Mountains.
