Father of [[Luna Blanchette]] and the don of the [[Sinclair Mob]].

Based out of the [[Miracle District]]. Amon is an archdevil from the [[Nine Hells]]. He once served in [[Asmodeus]]’ Court until he was caught embezzling souls and amassing soldiers for his own cause. Amon aimed to stage a coup against the Lord of the Hells, but was found out by an unnamed pit fiend. Betraying Asmodues is not an act that would be taken lightly, and Amon felt every scorn of punishment. His wings were torn from his body, he was striped of his powers and status then  banished to the martial plane with a task. Collect 1,000,000 mortal souls for the King of the Hells and have a chance at redemption, failure to do this and he will waste away a nobody in a sea of eternal damnation. None of these souls he could keep for himself. However, he was able to keep his Charisma. This allowed him to quickly earn him a following, which later formed into the Sinclair Mob.
