Bitter rival of [[Stuff]].
Both trained underneath their master, [[Quan-Xi]] in the [[Way of Fung]].
Thinkle "Things" spent his entire life living in the shadow of [[Stuff]], fostering a bitter grudge and hatred.
He avowed to do something drastic to make his name remembered forever and not laughed at.
Was killed by [[Jon Johnson (Abe Stone)]], during the [[Stufflock 2 Rat Thing Incident]].

9/16
=
Things was coming back from a date that went badly, when he saw [[Big Harbin]] flying in the sky.
Suddenly, a cloak landed at his feet, the [[Cloak of the Rat King]], belonging to the former [[Ringo (Rat King)]].
	The cloak granted him control over rats.
At this point, Things began to come up with a plan.
He spent the next 9 months devising a plan to cyphon the gas from the [[Heritage District]] and [[Miracle District]], then command an army of rats to set off a chain of explosions with various matches and lighters.
