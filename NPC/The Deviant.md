Member of the [[Lost Lineage]]


## Relationship to Lost Lineage
The Deviant has declined an invitation to the [[Lost Lineage]] in [[Lost Linage Letters]]. He cited this being due to being *"To preoccupied in his own spheres of influence."* and he *"Could muster that sort of  affluence in mere days"*

However after being visited by [[Abba Fuller (The Fool)]] and arriving to a compromise, The Deviant has since joined the organization.



## Relation to [[Richard Innsmouth (The Scripter)]]
Having in his letter acknowledge that he already knew about the death of [[Aurora Innsmouth]], expressing his sympathies, and the implication he wrote Richard's name before whiting it out and replacing it with "The Scripter"; all suggests The Deviant is someone who knows Richard well
