Punisher of Greed and Lord of Wealth in the [[Nine Hells]].
	Formely one of the Four Generals.
	"Friend" of [[Asmodeus]].
patron of [[Eclipse the Hedgehog]].
	Pazuzu is a being of pure chaos and hopes that Eclipse's reckless use of devilish powers will ignite another Blood War.
	Originally a general in the Blood Wars, Pazuzu's reckless battle tactics led him to being demoted to Lord of Wealth and punisher for the sin of greed.
	Craving more power, he now is trying to stoke chaos in the material plane as a means of climbing the satanic ladder.
