Lain is a 14 year old human girl from the Miracle District.

After ingesting a continues amount of contaminated water, she quickly grew ill and her organs began to fail her. Her mother [[Elaine]] had unknowingly contaminated her soup and the prolonged ingestion lead to Lain's fatal sickness. 

Lain was going to die, her organs had already failed, she had mere hours to live. The arcane operation that could rejuvenate her was far too expensive  Her mother however was approached by the M4C. They said they had a secret project that could preserve her daughter's soul while her body was healed.  This was [[Project RUST]]. 

Lain was sealed in a metal body for 19 months before being freed by her medical doctor [[Abraham Callistus]] and several member of PIBB, [[Miriella Honza]], [[Luther Du'Mont]], [[DarkNight GrimReaper]], [[Ghost]]
