Female Fallen Aasimar, formally the leader of the [[Vultura]]. She is imprisoned at [[Aground Penitentiary]]. Held on murder and plotting to assassinate high-profile characters.

Valka created the Vultura to help get vengeance on those who are corrupt or hurt others. Corrupt politicians or those who have hurt others. 

She is currently living an undeath after being killed by [[Sufjan (The Corpse)]] only then to be resurrected by [[Wee Jas]].
