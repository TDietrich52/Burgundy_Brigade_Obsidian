## Relationship to Richard
It is established that Richard expressed some form of hesitancy to work with The Fool when Richard states that his plans "include the [[Lost Lineage]] in those compatriots, *even the Fool.*" 
	The fool has previously used her jesters to attempt to kidnap [[Aurora Innsmouth]].

Richard appears to be aware that the fool can create and control her own [[The Jesters]] similar to the [[Crimson King]]
	*"The Fool doesn't control all [[The Jesters]], She makes her own" - [[Richard Innsmouth (The Scripter)]]*,


## The Fool's Alias (Abba Fuller)
Abba Fuller, celebrity entertainer represented by [[Polo Dubois]], helped [[Jeremiah Melinora (Jerma)]] steal the voices of others in the entertainment industry using her Jesters. She let her own voice be taken as well to avoid suspicion, and successfully wooed [[Aurelio Chaves]] during the investigation.
