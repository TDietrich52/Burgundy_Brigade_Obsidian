

## Basic Information
Edgar Solomon was the son of the perpetrators of the [[Solomon Manor]] Incident, though he was away at the [[DPA]] during this time. He was present and assisted [[PIBB]] during the investigation following [[Aurora Innsmouth]] killing several children.

After leaving the DPA, Edgar worked at [[Burgundy Bugle]] and continued to serve as a personal contact for [[DarkNight GrimReaper]].


## Death
Edgar died at the [[Crow's Nest]], in the presence of [[PIBB]] after being held captive by [[Doug Chaves]], who had the intention to have [[Mogwaii]] use Edgar's body to produce more mind flayers.
