Father of [[Aurelio Chaves]]
Former head of the Burgundy Police Department. 

Allied himself with the Mindflayer known as [[Mogwaii]]. Hoped Mogwaii would cleanse the street of crime by harnessing the psychic power of their Elder Brain monster. 

This conspiracy was uncovered by [[Edgar Soloman]] however and lead to the young reporter's death.
PIBB came to assist their ally and revealed Mogwaii. In the resulting battle Doug was wounded but escaped.
