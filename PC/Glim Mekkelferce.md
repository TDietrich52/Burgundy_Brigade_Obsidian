Saw a vision of Richard Innsmouth talking to a Crow which she recognized as [[Crow Rondel]], her adopted father.
# Class.
- Grave Domain Cleric.
	- From a young age, Glim has been training to enter the priesthood of the Crows Nest.
	- Upon reaching the age of 30, She finally completed all the necessary training and magical preparations to become a Priest of Wee Jas.  Included in this training was extensive medical study in which Glim became an expert in dissection, autopsy, surgery, and medicinal cures.  Additionally, Glim, like all Priests of Wee Jas, has been taught to normalize themselves with even the more horrific and gruesome of deaths in order to unlearn the principle that death is something scary to be avoided.
	- Through her belief and Wee Jas and extensive training and personal growth, Glim, as a member of the Crows, is able to channel a portion of Wee Jas’s power in order to interact with the forces of life and death.  When Glim casts a spell, she does not view the results of the spell as having originated from her, but rather being Wee Jas working through her to accomplish the goddess’s larger vision.

## Personality
- After spending years being taught magic, dark arts, religious beliefs, and medical practices, Glim has had very little social interaction with the outside world beyond the context of her role as a priest of Wee Jas.
	- As such her skills of social deduction and interaction are severely lacking.
* While she has the wisdom to be able to pick up on subtle changes in others and the environment, she lacks the awareness and self control to not immediately speak her mind about someone or something, regardless of the consequences.
- As such she can come across as tactless or snarky when in actuality she may genuinely be trying to be sincere or earnest.  This, combined with her dry wit and sense of humor has led to her often being the straight man of the group who looks out for people from the back.
- She has also been described as having a motherly air about her.
