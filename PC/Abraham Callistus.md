Goes by Abi or Dr. Callistus. Works at the [[Burgundy Medical Institute]]. He came to Burgundy from the [[Feywild]]. Learned his craft at [[BUAT]]. Hired by [[Ludwig Afton]] for [[Project RUST]] For a year he worked for Ludwig and the [[M4C (Revachol Industries)]]. He worked to put Lain Mieora's concioussness into a robot. They succeeded, but Lain was not able to portray complex emotions. He worked to try and fix his mistake. After the fall of M4 he met [[PIBB]]. They helped him to restore [[Lain]].
