## History
* Shaggy's mother raced in [[The Ridiculous Roller Derby]] where she encounted [[Dragula Rayser]].
	* According to Shaggy's father, Casey Lillard Watson, Dragula cursed his mother with lycanthropy for spurning Dragula's advances.
	* In actuality, Shaggy's mom just kinda got Lycanthropy from sleeping with a werewolf after the race, but she didn't want to her toxic husband to find out so she made up a story about being cursed by Dragula.
	* Shortly afterwards, she gave birth to Shaggy.
* Shaggy's father was ashamed of his son and sent him to military school to try and straighten him out.
	* Shaggy, a perpetual coward, ran away at the first sign of conflict and never looked back.
	* While on the run, he ran into the Scabby Gang and decided to form a mystery solving group with them.
* To earn some money, Shaggy decided to enter [[The Ridiculous Roller Derby]], where he first met [[Carlisle]] and Dragula.
	* Carlisle and Shaggy quickly hit it off, starting a wild and often frot on again off again relationship.
	* Dragula was impressed by Shaggy's driving skills, but Shaggy, believing his mother's stories, was scared out of his mind of Dragula and ran away.
* Shaggy has been to the [[Ratacombs]], where he sometimes goes to wind down after a sufficiently intense bender.
	* Shaggy told [[Carlisle]] about the Ratacombs but Carlisle didn't believe him.
