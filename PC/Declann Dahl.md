Famed owner of DD Tailor in Burgundy. 
DD Tailorship, is widely known as the best luxury tailor business in Burgundy. Declann has made suits and dresses to many of the most influential people in the city, the Mayor, Luna Blanchette, and others.

Lived in [[Tenenbaum]] as a baby.

## Dealings With A Devil 
Declann was doing his thing at D.D. Tailoring in Burgundy. He thought himself to be the best in the city, and he declared himself that on most occasions he could. However, his perception of himself would change when he was visited by a Pit Fiend in his home one night. The Pit Fiend was a servant of [[Mephistopheles]], the Grand Mage of the Nine Hells, and magical advisor to Asmodeus himself. The Pit Fiend demanded Declann to come with him, Declann, horrified at the monster as well as fearing for his life and soul, obliged the fiend and was brought to Mephistopheles' wizard tower. Mephistopheles chastised Declann for his pride and vanity, and demanded Declann prove his words by making the archdevil a suit, a pair of dress pants, and a robe. Each will be judged by Mephistopheles and his court of devils for their quality. Each must be perfect or Declann would loose his hands, mouth, and soul for eternity. Declann realizing where his pride had gotten him, began making the only outfit that ever truly matter, the only one that would determine the outcome of his enteral afterlife. Through stress and pushing his very nature as a Foxkin, Delcann produced the outfit to the Grand Mage of the Nine Hells. Mephistopheles was impressed by Declann's hard work. He saw the work as acquit and close enough to perfection as a mortal could craft, and without the assistance of magic no less.

Mephistopheles gifted Declann a short dagger as a boon and reminder of the experience hes been through, he warned Declann to not let his pride encase him again, or Mephistopheles will visit him again, but with no second chance this time. Since then Declann has held the dagger close to him to keep him on track and to keep himself humble for what hes been through.
