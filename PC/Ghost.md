Real name: Garmr Vinet

## Summery
Life was within the borders of normal before the Rat King Incident, however the loss of his partner Jack has inspired a new life path for the now closed off wolf walker. Now stalking the streets of Burgundy, Ghost has abandoned his life of the arts for a search for justice. 

After the vigilant incident, Ghost now will work missions with his close ally DarkNight, and following the M4 one-shot - often uses his time to spy on the company ensuring they never create artificial life and uphold their promise of preserving the Proto-type A Atomitons safe.
