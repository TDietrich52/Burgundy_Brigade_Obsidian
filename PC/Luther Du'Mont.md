## General
- Luther was raised in the [[The Du'Mont Family]]
- While investigating the death of his girlfriend, [[Lydia Gracefield]], was lead to ruins where he opened a coffin within a tomb and a creature inside "gave him life." 
- Since then he hears voices in strange languages, though he has never followed their commands.
- After her death he vowed to destroy whatever killed Lydia.

Currently has a crush on [[Luna Blanchette]]

# Background
## Powers
- Luther is still not entirely sure of the source of his powers.  He knows he has a guardian animal who acts as a guiding light, but other than that it's a mystery to him why he is connected to the [[Shadowfell]]. Thinks the connection comes from him opening an ancient tomb and creating a [[Demiplane of Dread]] from his grief and despair at losing his fiance Lydia, but his memory is hazy and the answers are unclear.

## Before PIBB
* Luther was raised by his father, Gregory Du'Mont, away from the rest of his family.
	* With his father being the head of criminal affairs for [[The Du'Mont Shipping Incorperated]], Luther was often exposed and normalized to criminal activities at a young age.
* Attended [[BUAT]], where he met [[Lydia Gracefield]] and joined the fencing team

## Relationship to the Du'Monts
* The relationship between Luther and the rest of his family has always been tense
	* Even at a young age, Luther understood that his family weren't good people, nor that they did good things.
	* It made him sick to think of all of the people they've exploited to get the wealth they now possess.
* The older he grew and the more he learned, the more sickened he became.
	* He enrolled in [[BUAT]] on a scholarship program for fencing entirely to try and avoid any association with his family.
		* He never once told the rest of his family if or where he was going to school.
	* However, he soon learned that you can't wash off the stink of a Du'Mont so easily.
		* At age 20, Luther was undisputed best fencer at the [[BUAT]] and aced his auditions for the Rainbow Coast Olympics.
		* One look at his last name, though, and the judges passed on him in favor of his second.
			* They told Luther to his face "The Du'Monts can buy all the wins they want in Burgundy, but the Rainbow Coast Commission has STANDARDS".
* That comment would trigger a downward spiral for Luther, leading him down a path of drugs and alcohol that seemed to end in one way and one way only.
	* And why should end any differently? He was a Du'Mont, no matter how much he fought it.
	* Maybe this was what he deserved.



	* When Walter Du'Mont Jr. passed away and left [[Castle Du'Mont]] to Luther, he considered selling it back to the city and keeping the money, but he and Lydia wanted a real home together. so they moved into the castle and began planning the start of their new life...
