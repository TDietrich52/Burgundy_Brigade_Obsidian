# General
## Background
* Volunteer Medical "Expert" at the [[Burgundy Medical Precinct Number 4]].
* Hadozee monk trained by Master Quan-Xi in the Way of Fung.
	* Specializes in using his powers to heal others.
* Was originally mistaken for a service animal when he was brought to the precinct.
	* when they realized he was sentient they hastily gave him an internship since they are woefully understaffed.

# Lore
## Mastering the [[Way of Fung]]
* Chaka fell from the tree tops as a little baby, falling out of his mother's grip and plummeting towards the ground.
* Thankfully, he survived and landed safely on the doorstep of Quan-Xi.
* Quan-Xi took pity on the poor child, whom he presumed abandoned by his family, and took him in and raised him in the ways of Fung martial arts.
* As Chaka grew, he proved particularly apt at channeling his ki into healing energy, so Quan-Xi also taught him herbal medicine and actupuncture to further supplement his medical prowess.
* Chaka does not know much about his homeworld and culture beyond what the average citizen would know.
	* He hopes to be able to return their one day and experience it for himself.
