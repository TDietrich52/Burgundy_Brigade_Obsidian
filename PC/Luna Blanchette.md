## Basic Biography
Luna Blanchette is a famous dancer, actor, and singer who is pioneering radio technology along the Rainbow Coast. She, on the side, works for [[PIBB]] as an investigator to fulfill her deal with Asmodeus.


### Asmodeus's Deal
Luna must to figure out what [[Deep Ones]] are and why their souls are never come to Asmodeus as they should. The slaying of Deep Ones is not required but is not discouraged.
