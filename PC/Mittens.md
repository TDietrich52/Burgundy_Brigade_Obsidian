## Basic Biography
Mittens started as a street performer playing his lute for change in the historical district. As a child he used to skip classes to go out and perform - using his lute as a distraction to slip his paws into the pockets of those better off than him. He was a child of the Burgundy Foster Care system, so there wasn’t anyone keeping too close an eye on him.

Once he aged out, he moved into the Miracle District and rented out a room for a nice older couple who never noticed their goods occasionally going missing. More than likely they were just happy having someone young in the house again and Mittens was more than happy with a cheap room.

It was soon after he left school Mittens found his only friend, Cryus. Cryus is the skull that shares it’s stories with Mittens, where Mittens gets his subclass abilities from. Mittens adores Cryus as one of the best treasure’s he’s stolen, and is a bit unaware he cares about the spirit within. 

Mittens continues to be a sus little guy on the streets of Burgundy stealing from folks to add to his growing hoard of junk he has stolen.
