# General
Sir Grunty, Prince of Toads.
	Seen through the eyes of [[Luther Du'Mont]].
Wicken during [[Old Burgundy (3669)]]
Bastardous prince turned Grung monster hunter.
	Died in combat with [[Crimson King]].

# Background
- Class.
	- Swarmkeeper Ranger. 
	- Born Prince Gunther, Sir Grunty received a top notch education in regards to chivalry, sword fighting, reading/writing, and the practice of hunting.  After being turned into a frog, Sir Grunty inherited some of the magic of the fey and used it to enhance his combat process.
	- He enhances those who fight alongside him with his magic to manipulate the battlefield, move with the shadows, and strike the enemies weak points.  He applies this knowledge for the good of the Rainbow Coast at great personal cost, as being a Wicken has cost him his love, his kingdom, and even his humanity.
- Personality.
	- Once a proud and haughty prince, being a Wickar has humbled Sir Grunty.  He gives of himself to those around him on the hope that said kindness will be reciprocated.  While his hopes are often dashed, he still pushes on, believing that everyone can have a second chance at life. 
	- Cheery on the outside, dark on the inside.  Still confident, sometimes his old arrogance bleeds through.  He is very respectful of authority (aside from people who worship the gods) and often more than willing to risk his life for the respect and adulation of others.  All this is to mask the pain and fear he feels for his past actions and how he pines for a redemption that he feels is always out of reach.
- Miscellaneous.
	- Theme: Frog Theme-Chrono Trigger.
	- Death Theme: Far off Promise-Chrono Trigger.

## History
* Killing a Monster.
	* In order to win a princess’s hand in marriage, Prince Gunther led his elite squadron of knights into battle against a Jabberwocky in the nearby Swamplands.
	* Everyone but Gunther died a horrible death, as Gunther fled deep into the trees for safety.
	* As penance, the gods transformed Gunther into a vile and detestable toad.
	* Gunther, now named Sir Grunty, seethed with rage and took his revenge on the Monster that killed his men.
	* After attacking from a distance, Grunty lured the monster deeper into the swamps, until it had been completely entangled by the marshy trees, prompting Grunty to pounce upon it from the treetops.
		* A fierce battle ensued, but at the end of it, Sir Grunty, beaten and bloodied, emerged victorious.
* The BladeBow.
	* Grunty tore out the Jabberwocky’s Heart and forged a new weapon with it out of his old bow and swords, one capable of killing monsters from a distance or up close.
	* And thus the BladeBow was forged.With this weapon in hand, Sir Grunty set out to become a monster hunter.
	* Over the years, Grunty has killed a number of monsters, ranging from witches to demons to even a few aberrations and dragonspawn.
	* The whereabouts of the Bladebow are currently unknown.
* Becoming a Wicken.
	* After killing the Monster, Grunty tore out its heart and offered it to the gods as a trophy in exchange for returning his humanity.  His prayers went unanswered and Grunty realized that his penance was not yet served.
	* Dejected, he returned home, but upon reaching the gates, the guards drove him away, thinking him a monster.  Made humble, Grunty was forced to accept that his old life was over, and that his only hope at redemption was to become a Wicken.
	* As the years went by, Grunty earned a reputation along the rainbow coast for his actions as a Wicken, earning the title of the Prince of Toads, which depending on who tells the story is either an insult or a compliment.
