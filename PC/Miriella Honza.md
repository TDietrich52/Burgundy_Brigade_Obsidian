- Was awaken to her [[Deep Ones]] form by [[Atticus McGraw (The Boffin)]]

- Has carved the rune of her mother into her palm, that aids her in shifting to her Deep One form

### The Whalers Dream
While in [[Chandelle Bookkeep]] was shown this panel of wood: 
-   The wood is a small fragment of a whaling boat that washed up on the southern rainbow coast.
-   Contains information relating to a whaler and what he saw on a boat.
-   The whaler mentions a woman under the water, and feels called to her.  He describes her with the skin of fish, hands of silk, and white like the full moons. 
-   Carved at the bottom is a spider-like rune that in deep speech means "mothering"
-   Ask about the Mother of Deep Ones.  the rune again ignites.
-   The rune pertains specifically to the process of mothering, not necessarily a mother.
