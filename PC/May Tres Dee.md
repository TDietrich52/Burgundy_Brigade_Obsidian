## General
Maitre'd at the [[Gourm Clair De Lune]]
Adopted daughter of the Claire De Lune's owner.
	They adopted her as she was originally in werewolf form as a child when [[Chef Meme]] found her and raised her, and the owner was afraid of being accused of using child labor if anyone found out.
	Lives with [[Chef Meme]] who is like a father figure to her.
May is a chameleon tiefling who can transform into a werewolf as a result of drinking a Witches Brew as a child.
Unbeknownst to anyone, May is secretly the long lost heir of the Blight Family.
	The Blight Family were a family of human supremacists that abandoned May as a baby.
	They have no living human heirs.
	In the winter of 6664, May’s biological mother and last recognized member of the Blight Family, Regina Blight, died of health complications at the age of 55, leaving the Blight estate without an official heir.
		The driver Leroy Matthews, now an elderly man in his 70s, admitted that the Blights did indeed have a daughter and that, despite her abandonment, she was the rightful heir to the Blight name and fortune, or what little was left that hadn’t been pissed away.
		And so, once spring came in the year 6665, Leroy led a search to find Astaro Blight and have her named the rightful heir to the Blight family.
		However, after a full year and some change of searching, nothing has turned up, causing many to believe that Astaro died a long time ago…

## Personality
* May has taken the Clare De Lune motto, “Sophistication, served fresh and exotic”, to heart as her unifying personal philosophy.  To her the customer is always right, no matter how absurd or ridiculous the demand is.  This, combined with her experience as an orphan on the streets, has led her to put up a strong veneer of politeness, constantly smiling and presenting a reassuring and pleasant energy.
- However, occasionally snippets of her true personality peek through, such as the condescending tone she often speaks in or the way she dominates those in a lower position in the restaurant or those who she has no fear/respect for.  This hint at her true feelings most often comes up whenever her skin changes color based on her emotions (this was a lot clearer before the world turned black and white).  It is not until she becomes a werewolf that May ever fully speaks her mind.
- Despite being in full control of her werewolf side, some her animalistic instincts have bled into May’s everyday life, resulting in such behaviors as having a strong dislike for all cats and catlike humanoid, her wagging her tail upon receiving compliments or praise, and her overzealous energy coming on display when she partakes in her passions/hobbies.
