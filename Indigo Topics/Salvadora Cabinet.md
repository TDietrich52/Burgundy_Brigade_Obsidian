#Indigo | The ruling body of [[Indigo]]


#### Establishment
Following the [[Nights of Black Fire]], and with no established government, a majority of citizens supported [[The Salvadora Sisters]] (Year 6502). Hosted at the [[Radiant Tear Cathedral]]

#### Organization
Annually the Cabinet is reorganized. However, there is no term limit and as such roles may go unchanged for long periods. Cabinet members must be approved by all three sisters.
