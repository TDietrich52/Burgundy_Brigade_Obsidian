#Indigo  | The youngest of [[The Salvadora Sisters]]

### During The Nights of Black Fire
Madria was born shortly before the [[Nights of Black Fire]]. Only being a newborn at the time. During the conflict she was wounded, becoming blind and crippled, but thanks to Anathesys, she survived the massacre. 

### Modern Era
Madria is the Head Cardinal of [[Church of Anathesys]]. She is thought to be the closest conduit to [[Anathesys]]. She is rarely seen outside her quarters in the [[Radiant Tear Cathedral]].
