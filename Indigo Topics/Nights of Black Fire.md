#Indigo Year 6499

[[Indigo]] was attacked by a group of death conspiracists - by [[The Blackstars]]
Many Influential leaders were killed in their sleep, some by [[Lich King Ygoro]] himself

The name "Nights of Black Fire", refers to the Necrotic Plames that burned the city
After 6 days, the hopelessness was ended by [[The Salvadora Sisters]]

#### The Night of Wailing
This refers to the first night of the Nights of Black Fire, when the attacks were first launched

#### Ygoro's Massacre
This attack was planned months in advance, with Ygoro forged special daggers called The [[Black Blades]]
##### Targets Included
- Former Prime Minister Emily Mementa
- Mementa’s husband and three children
- Head Officer Kiren Diaz
- Eight other high ranking guard officers
- Prime Cleric Primish Natalyn 
- Four high ranking medical officers


#### The Defense of the [[Radiant Tear Cathedral]]
- Called **Loretta Cathedral** at the time of Nights of Black Fire
- Main line of defense against King Ygoro’s attack, keeping thousands safe

#### The Night of Everlight
- On this day [[Anathesys]], through [[Amelia Salvadora]], light the night sky entirely 
- [[Lich King Ygoro]] and his forces were defeated by the radiant light
	-  It's said that Pegasus descended from the heavens tramping the thralls of the tarnished The Lich King and his undead army
	- Lich King Ygoro was said to be executed by [[Amelia Salvadora]], with [[Aria Salvadora]] and several key generals there to bear witness
