#Indigo  was the origin for commercial air travel!

A travel method spanning from Indigo to its neighbors along the Rainbow Coast with ports in Burgundy, Marigold and Cerulea. In the coming years, they are looking into expanding their operations to even more cities having only started the company ~10 years ago.

*"Thanks to [[Salvadora Cabinet]], we are now able to freely travel the skies. I think a world of airship travel the future, and I will work to see this future is in our lifetime."* - Tamino Volar (Founder of Indigo Express)

### Effectiveness
From Burgundy to [[Indigo]] is ~4-5 day travel.
Storms will slow airship downs, making them impractical during the rainy seasons



### Trivia
- Airship technology is fairly new- being about 30-40 years old
