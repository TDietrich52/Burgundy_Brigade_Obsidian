The Radiant Tear Cathedral in #Indigo 

The [[Radiant Tear Cathedral]] is the headquarters of the [[Church of Anathesys]] and the home of Indigo's government - [[Salvadora Cabinet]].  



## During the [[Nights of Black Fire]]
- Pre-Nights of Black Fire the Church was called **Loretta Cathedral**
- Served as the primary defense against [[Lich King Ygoro]] until the [[The Salvadora Sisters]] emerged
- After the attack the Cathedral was renamed **Radiant Tear Cathedral** and now was remodeled by the [[Salvadora Cabinet]] to serve as a memorial to those who were lost


## Trivia
- It has since become  the crown jewel of Indigo with its highest spire reaching 504 feet tall
	- Massive 2500' x 2500' complex consisting of a palace, church, and fortress.
