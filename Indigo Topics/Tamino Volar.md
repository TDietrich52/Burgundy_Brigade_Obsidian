## Basic Overview
Tamino is a gnomish woman and inventor. Founder of [[Gnometown Burrow]] in Indigo and the CEO of the [[Indigo Express Company]].

Earned her position as infrastructure head in the cabinet after proposing and engineering the modern  [[Indigonese Tram Service (ITS)]]. The ITS proved to be such a success, [[Amelia Salvadora]] installed Tamino in the cabinet only a month later.


## Personality
Known to get caught up in personal and expensive pet projects.
