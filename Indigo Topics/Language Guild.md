Group in #Indigo  
Headed by [[Eneko Barillas]]


Citizens are very much encouraged to learn new languages. This is thanks to the Indigonese Language Guilds. Languages such as Common, Elven, Gnomish, and Dwarven are all spoken abundantly in Indigo and many are multi-lingual in a combination of those. The Indigonese Language Guilds provide many educational resources available to aid citizens in learning more exotic languages such as Celestial, Draconic, Giant. There are even niche languages featured such as Goblin, Aarakocra, and Sylvan. 

This, along with the prominent use of Celestial in worship of [[Anathesys]], has led Celestial to be one of Indigo's most prominent languages. Infernal and Abyssal are forbidden to be learned, viewed to be a corruptive force because of their ties to demonic forces.
