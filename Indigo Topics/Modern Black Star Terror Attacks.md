#Indigo 

### Summery
Recently Indigo has experienced several necromancy based events that have lead [[The Salvadora Sisters]] to suspect that the [[The Blackstars]] are attempting to resurrect [[Lich King Ygoro]]. This has lead to [[Anathesys]] calling [[PIBB]] here.



### Incidents In Order (And Effected Committee Members)
1. A maternity ward lost each child born that, necrotic magic was present. The Ward was overseen by [[Alejandro Lazaretto]]. *Note: 46 children were lost.*

2. Several thousand dead fish washed ashore in Indigo Harbor a Black Star Rune (see [[The Blackstars]]) was found carved into each's innards. The Pier was being overseen by [[Tamino Volar]](?)

3. A homeless shelter (in the [[Cinder Burrow]]) was hit by the same plague found in the fish.  The Black Star Rune carved into patient zero’s chest. The shelter was overseen by [[Dezolé Diawara]]

4. A Bank teller, Denirr Tucco, was burned alive by a Black Flame - a rune by [[The Blackstars]] was found on the bill he had been holding. The bank was overseen by [[Abastos Moore]]. *Noted: Denirr survived this*

5. Sellia Toth, faculty member of the [[Rennala Academy of the Arcane]] and member of the [[Language Guild]] was found dead at the Academy's library, killed by a [[Black Blades]]. The Academy is overseen by [[Ria Lazarus]]

6.  [[Sierra Ciega]]'s Blackstar rune rapidly grew, and her death quickly followed. *Note: Death occurred due to heart attack - but with the Blackstar's rate of growth she would have been dead in about 48 hours regardless*
