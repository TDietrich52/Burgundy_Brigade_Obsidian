Mountain range along the boarder of [[Indigo]] | #Indigo 


The mountain's peaks can reach temperatures of 70-80 degrees even in the middle of winter. This clash in temperatures create great and thick fog storms during the cooler months.

Named for its bizarre climate system, the higher you climb the warmer the temperatures become.
- Indigenous meteorologists theorize the mountain range might actually be a dormant volcano, with small caverns seeping out hot air. 
-  Others question this theory as Indigo is not prone to tremors or ground quakes.
