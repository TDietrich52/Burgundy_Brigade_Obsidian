#Indigo  

Serves on the [[Salvadora Cabinet]] as *Magistrate of the Judgement Choir*


## Overview
Hornless Tiefling - lost in the [[Nights of Black Fire]]. 
Over 170 and relatively private-  longest serving cabinet member and one of the closest to the [[The Salvadora Sisters]]

Sierra is the magistrate of the Judgment Choir, a group of anonymous lawyers and judges dedicated to upholding peace in the city of Indigo. Sierra and her choir are in charge of the courts of Indigo.
