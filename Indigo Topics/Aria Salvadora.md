### Historical Contributions
Following The [[Nights of Black Fire]], Aria took control of the remaining guard population, training them to harness [[Anathesys]]'s power for combat. Creating battalions of Clerics and Paladins all followers of Anathesys. She is known to have ==personally killed [[Lich King Ygoro]].==

### Modern Goals
Aria's job in the modern day is to police and protect the city of Indigo and its people. She is the commander in chief of the city guard and special commission of securities. Her title being ==Head of Police and Overseer of [[The Inquisition]]==.


*Suspects [[Lich King Ygoro]] May have active followers within Indigo*
