- From Burgundy -  only cabinet member not from Indigo.
- Successful architect and designed many of Burgundy’s most well known casinos, theaters, and banks. Then became a very successful investor and accountant. At the age of 40 he was one of the richest men on the Rainbow Coast, but he donated his wealth through philanthropy. 
- Upon moving to Indigo, he was quickly given a role in the [[Salvadora Cabinet]]


Tends to be very frugal with the budget. 
Supports the idea of adding Infernal to the [[Language Guild]]
