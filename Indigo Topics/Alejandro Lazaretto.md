#Indigo 

Member of [[Salvadora Cabinet]], serving as *Minister of Medicine and Healing*

![[Pasted image 20230604192438.png | 300]]

## Overview 
- appointed shortly following [[The Noir]] in 6665
- Alejandro is a human man without any magical abilities of his own.

Despite this handicap, excelled in medicine
	- Performed the first brain tumor removal without the use of magic–a pediatric surgery
	- This feat impressed [[Amelia Salvadora]] tremendously, and she recommended he be placed in the cabinet for a time. 

So far, Alejandro has championed surgical research and non-magical training to broaden practitioners within the field.


### Relationship to Other Cabinet Members
- Expressed a level of resentment for [[Tamino Volar]]
